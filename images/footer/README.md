### Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a single finished landscape illustration for the closing banner of a Japanese-inspired academic personal website.
Primary request: a beautiful, restrained Japanese coastal railway scene, warm and airy, with an unusually tasteful gray-purple palette that complements an ivory page and deep plum typography.
Scene: quiet coast seen from a slightly elevated viewpoint, a very small vintage Japanese electric railcar traveling along a gently curving shoreline railway in the lower right third, open sea stretching into the distance. The train is a tiny part of the landscape, not a character or product. A few natural coastal grasses in the near foreground. No people.
Style/medium: refined contemporary Japanese editorial illustration, delicate pencil contours and matte gouache, subtle printed-paper grain, softly layered shapes with convincing landscape depth. A sophisticated illustrated travel journal, clean and composed, not a cartoon, not pixel art, not photorealistic, not a UI mockup.
Composition: very wide panoramic landscape, approximately 2.4:1 aspect ratio. Give the sea and pale sky most of the picture. Keep all essential details in the middle 75 percent so mild responsive cropping works. Sky occupies the top 40 percent, coastline and small train the bottom half. Asymmetric composition and generous breathing room; a clear, beautiful image with enough detail to enjoy close up.
Color palette: ivory and warm off-white sky, muted lavender-gray and slate blue sea, deeper plum and dusty violet for the train and small shadows, restrained desaturated sage in grasses. Gentle luminous late-afternoon daylight, neutral highlights, no golden sun or strong orange, no dark night. Low saturation but rich dark details, no oversaturated pink.
Constraints: no text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no border or website frame. No rock garden, no raked sand, no sunrise snow mountain. Produce only the finished illustration.

### Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: one editorial photograph for the footer of a Japanese-inspired academic personal website.
Primary request: a quiet Japanese karesansui dry garden in softly overcast daylight, photographed with restraint and beautiful real material detail.
Subject and composition: vertical 4:5 composition, a small asymmetrical group of three dark weathered natural stones in pale ivory raked gravel, gentle curved rake lines flowing around stones, a small low island of muted moss, a few delicate Japanese maple leaves intruding from the upper right edge; close intimate garden crop, no mountains, no buildings, no people. Open gravel in the lower half creates visual breathing room. View slightly from above, real garden scale, editorial architecture photography.
Color palette: warm ivory gravel (#f4f0e6), dark ink shadows with a subtle deep aubergine bias (#382638), restrained olive moss, natural pale gray stone. The site uses strong Edo purple #603780 as an accent, so image should harmonize through warm neutrals and deep shadows, not purple-tinted plants.
Lighting/mood: soft diffused light, tactile stone and sand, peaceful, subtle photographic grain, realistic matte color, enough contrast to avoid looking washed out.
Constraints: the photograph only, edge-to-edge composition, no text, no calligraphy, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no red sun, no cherry blossom spectacle, no artificial colored lighting, no fantasy landscape.
