I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang University, supervised by Prof. Dongxiang Zhang. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Xidian University (2022-2026), where I worked with Dr. Juli Zhang.

I study multimodal learning, particularly concept drift, anomaly detection, and AI for Science. I am especially interested in building robust and trustworthy models that remain reliable as data distributions change in real-world applications. I welcome discussions and collaborations on these topics. Feel free to reach out by email.
